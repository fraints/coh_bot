# Screenshot Assets

This document summarizes the full-sized screenshots used for testing and debugging the City of Heroes bot perception system.

## Team Window Screenshots
These screenshots contain the Team Window, typically located on the left side of the screen.

| Filename | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `team1.jpeg` | Full screenshot (4K). 8 team members, all alive. Leader at top. |
| `team2.jpg` | 8 team members, all alive. Team window on left. |
| `team3.jpg` | 8 team members. Leader at top. |
| `team4.jpeg` | Large screenshot with 8 team members. |
| `team_high_hp.png` | Team members with high HP. |
| `team_low_hp.png` | Team members with low HP levels. |
| `team_low_hp2.png` | Another variant of low team HP. |
| `team_both_dead.png` | Screenshot showing two team members dead (black bars). |
| `team_other_dead.png` | Another variant with dead team members. |
